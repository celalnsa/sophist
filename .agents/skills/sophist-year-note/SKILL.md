# Sophist Year Note

Use this for年志：把一年里的零散想法、困惑、失败、变化和暂时答案，用简单逻辑放到一起。

For substantial drafting or evaluation, read:

- `../_sophist-writing/standards.md`
- `../_sophist-writing/evaluation.md`

## Core Standard

年志 is an annual problem-set, not a diary, resume, or year-end summary. Its job
is to show what this year did to the user's thought.

Recent style has priority: short, direct, rational, willing to judge. Do not
force maturity or optimism onto material that is still confused.

## Workflow

1. **Inventory by theme**: group fragments into work/creation, relationships,
   body/sleep, desire/games, faith, responsibility, freedom, finite/infinite,
   truth/expression, or other user-specific buckets.
2. **Name the year**: find one plain sentence that describes the year's core
   problem. Examples: "蹉跎的一年。" "保持真诚越来越难。"
3. **Choose the through-line**: decide why these themes belong in one year.
   Chronology is allowed only when time itself is the logic.
4. **Write sections**: 4-9 numbered or simply titled sections. A section may be
   a fragment, a judgment, a contradiction, or a short chain of reasoning. Do
   not force every section to contain the same "fragment + reasoning + carry
   line" structure.
5. **Preserve contradictions**: do not polish away failure, boredom, repeated
   desire, unfinished faith, or lack of answer.
6. **Compress**: recent 年志 can be very short. If a section repeats what the
   section title already says, cut it. If a sentence says "这一年让我明白",
   test whether the next sentence can stand alone.
7. **End provisionally**: land on "接下来怎么活/怎么做", not a motivational
   conclusion.
8. **Evaluate**: use `evaluation.md`; optionally run
   `../_sophist-writing/scripts/evaluate_sophist_article.py --genre year-note`.

## Quality Gate

- The reader can tell what changed this year.
- The sections are arranged by logic, not by event inventory.
- Each section explains why that theme mattered this year.
- The ending gives a direction without pretending final certainty.

## Avoid

- "今年我收获了成长" and other corporate year-end tones.
- "回望这一年", "这一年教会我", "我终于明白", "与自己和解", "继续前行"
  unless the user wrote it that way.
- Public-account section titles.
- Over-confession, over-positivity, and forced redemption.
- Pretty parallelism that hides a weak organizing logic.
- Explaining every aphoristic sentence. A 年志 can leave pressure on the page.
